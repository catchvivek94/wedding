On site

  • Valley views from most rooms
  • Outdoor pool and lawn space for functions
  • Two restaurants, including Rasa
  • Spa & wellness centre
  • Fitness centre
  • Open ground for outdoor games and activities

Public reviews consistently praise the food, the valley views and the spa. The recurring complaint is that the pool is smaller than expected — worth seeing yourself if a pool function is in the plan.

Still to nail down

The booking is done — these are the operational answers you'll need from the resort before your planner can build a run sheet. Track them as tasks below.

  • Noise curfew — hill stations usually have one, and it decides when the sangeet has to stop
  • Function spaces — which space for the mandap, sangeet and reception, and the seated capacity of each
  • Room block — how many rooms you're holding, and the date unsold ones get released
  • Vendor load-in — can decor start setting up the day before, and is there a preferred-vendor list
  • Catering — in-house kitchen or outside caterer, and the corkage / liquor-licence position
  • Wet-weather plan — where each outdoor function moves to, and who calls it
  • Backup power — and whether it covers the sound and lighting rig
  • Payment schedule — instalment dates and what triggers each one
  • On-site contact — the name and mobile of whoever runs your two days
